# Original room editor illustrations

These seven furniture illustrations were created specifically for Mekan AI with original SVG geometry. They are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**, dedicating them to the public domain to the fullest extent permitted by law. You may copy, modify and redistribute them, including commercially, without attribution. See https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ for the legal instrument.

All assets use `viewBox="0 0 240 180"`, transparent backgrounds, ivory/earth/gold/charcoal colors and self-contained geometry. No external images, scripts, fonts or filters are required; they can be decoded by a browser image and drawn into a canvas.

They are generic illustrative overlays, not photographs or exact representations of products, manufacturers, physical dimensions or validated 3D objects. Marketplace product associations remain links to real catalog records; the overlay itself does not prove visual or dimensional fidelity.
