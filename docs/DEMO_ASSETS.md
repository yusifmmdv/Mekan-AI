# Prepared AI examples and provenance

These are repository-owned **prepared examples**, generated with Codex's built-in imagegen tool in this session. That tool is not the runtime Qwen provider. No `OPENAI_API_KEY` is used by the application to create or display these files. They are not photographs of a customer's real room or evidence of a successful runtime API integration.

Files: `public/demo/empty-room.png`, `home.png`, `office.png`, `studio.png`. The three furnished outputs edit the same generated empty-room source. All four final images were visually inspected. The source has a left black-framed window, rear white wall, right white door and oak floor. Saved examples retain these architectural elements. Furniture regions were manually annotated after inspection; they are not claimed as detector output. Imported examples store `provider=demo`, `model=builtin-imagegen`, `preparedDemo=true`, `annotatedBy=human`, and the sample ID. Live generations use independent provider metadata and automated detection.

Product thumbnail SVGs are original CC0 illustrations from the earlier asset collection. They are catalogue illustrations; they do not represent exact product photography. Example prices and supplier names are explicitly demo data. Private projects resolve their associated products against current approved catalogue records.

A large black framed two-pane window on the LEFT wall admits warm daylight, blank warm-white rear wall, light oak wood floor, clean white ceiling. On the right wall near the rear a simple closed white door. Absolutely no furniture, no people, no objects, no text or watermark. Architectural photography, realistic lighting, elegant generous floor space, sharp high resolution.

**Home edit:** Edit this exact empty room into a stunning photorealistic warm Scandinavian living room for a home interior design demonstration. Output landscape 3:2 same framing as original.
